# Progress updates and demos

**Status: proposal.** Format and cadence are open for discussion. Open an issue or a pull request to change them.

## Written updates

Every two weeks, while a project is active, its maintainer updates the Status section of its project note in `projects/` by pull request: what changed, what is blocked, what is next, in three to five lines. Findings go in their own files, not in the update.

The pull request history of `projects/` is then the progress log of the whole organisation, readable by people and by agents.

## Demos

A monthly open call. Each active project gets up to ten minutes: one result or one failure, shown running, and one question for the room. Recordings and slides are linked from the project note.

## Why this shape

- Short and regular beats long and rare. Two weeks is enough for a spike to produce something, or to learn it will not.
- Written first, so teams in other time zones and agents can follow without the call.
- Failures get demo time. A negative result shown early saves another team the same month.
